# Season of Us

*A story of love, friendship, school life, and the choices that change everything.*

## About the Game

Season of Us is a story-based visual novel that follows the lives of three students: Aarav, Sia, and Rohan.

The story begins in Grade 8, where ordinary school days slowly turn into something more meaningful. As the characters grow older, they experience friendship, relationships, academic pressure, misunderstandings, and different moments that shape their lives.

The game has two different modes. Both follow the same characters, but their stories take different directions and lead to different endings.

## Game Modes

### 1. Normal Mode

Normal Mode tells the story of Aarav and Sia without betrayal or cheating. It follows their relationship from the day they first meet in Grade 8 to the different stages of their lives.

They start as classmates, become close friends, and eventually develop feelings for each other. Like any relationship, they have small arguments and disagreements, but they learn to understand and support each other.

As they reach Grade 10, academic pressure increases. They study together, compete for better marks, and prepare for their board exams. Aarav secures first place, while Sia comes second.

Even after leaving school, they stay together and continue building their future. The story eventually follows them years later, when they begin a new chapter of their lives together through marriage.

**Main themes:**
- Love and friendship
- Trust and understanding
- Academic life
- Growing together
- Supporting each other

### 2. Cheating Mode

Cheating Mode takes the story in a different direction. The relationship between Aarav and Sia faces betrayal, jealousy, and misunderstandings.

Things begin normally in Grade 8, but their relationship changes when Aarav discovers Sia with another boy in Grade 9. Despite being hurt, he gives the relationship another chance.

As they move into Grade 10, academic pressure and personal differences create more distance between them. Eventually, their relationship falls apart, and both have to move forward with their lives.

This mode explores what happens when things don't go as expected.

**Main themes:**
- Betrayal and trust
- Jealousy and misunderstandings
- Heartbreak
- Difficult decisions
- Moving on

## How to Install and Play

There are a few ways to get the game running on your computer.

### Option 1: Download the Project

1. Visit the [Season of Us GitHub repository](https://github.com/himanshusapkota/seasons-of-us).
2. Click the green **Code** button.
3. Select **Download ZIP**.
4. Extract the downloaded ZIP file.
5. Download and install the [Ren'Py SDK](https://www.renpy.org/latest.html).
6. Open the Ren'Py Launcher.
7. Select the extracted Season of Us project directory.
8. Click **Launch Project**.

The game should now start.

### Option 2: Clone Using Git

If you have Git installed, open your terminal or PowerShell and run:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/himanshusapkota/seasons-of-us.git
```

After cloning:

1. Open the Ren'Py Launcher.
2. Select the cloned project directory.
3. Click **Launch Project**.

Make sure you have the Ren'Py SDK installed before launching the project.

### Option 3: Play Directly in Your Browser

A browser version is planned so players can enjoy the game without installing Ren'Py.

Once the HTML5 version is built and hosted, you will be able to open the game through a website.
