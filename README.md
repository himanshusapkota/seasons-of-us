# Season of Us

*A story of young love, broken promises, and memories that never fade.*

## About

**Season of Us** is a visual novel that follows the journey of two high school students whose relationship begins in Grade 8. What starts as a beautiful teenage romance slowly turns into a story of heartbreak, misunderstandings, jealousy, and second chances.

Through different school years, the story explores how people change, how relationships are tested, and how sometimes the people we love don't stay with us forever.

## Story

### Grade 8 — The Beginning

Two students meet and fall in love. Everything feels perfect, and they believe their relationship will last forever. Their days are filled with happiness, memories, and promises about the future.

### Grade 9 — The First Betrayal

Things take an unexpected turn when the boy catches the girl with another boy. Heartbroken, he struggles to accept what happened. After desperately asking her to return, she eventually agrees, and they decide to give their relationship another chance.

### Grade 10 — Between Love and Dreams

Their relationship seems to be getting better, but board examination pressure begins to create distance between them.

The boy, being the school's top student, spends most of his time studying. Meanwhile, the girl feels neglected and becomes jealous of other couples who get to spend more time together. Eventually, she decides to end their relationship.

During the examinations, they find themselves seated next to each other. The girl secretly relies on his answers to improve her grades.

When the results are announced, the boy becomes the school topper, while the girl secures the second position.

### After Grade 10 — A New Beginning?

Both move to another school for further studies, hoping for a fresh start.

However, history begins to repeat itself. The girl develops feelings for another boy, leaving her previous relationship behind once again.

The boy is left heartbroken, realizing that the person he loved has chosen someone else.
