*A story of love, friendship, school life, and the choices that change everything.*

**[Play Online](https://season-of-us-hc.netlify.app/)**

## About

Season of Us is a story-based visual novel about three students: Aarav, Sia, and Rohan.

The story begins in Grade 8, where ordinary school days slowly turn into something more meaningful. As the characters grow older, they go through friendship, relationships, academic pressure, and misunderstandings.

The game has two modes. Both follow the same characters, but the stories take different directions and lead to different endings.

## Game Modes

### Normal Mode

Aarav and Sia's story without betrayal. They start as classmates, become close friends, and eventually fall for each other. In Grade 10 they study together for their board exams, then keep building their future after school.

**Themes:** love, trust, academic life, growing together.

### Cheating Mode

The same start, but a different road. In Grade 9, Aarav discovers Sia with another boy and chooses to give the relationship another chance. Pressure and personal differences pull them apart in Grade 10, and both have to move on.

**Themes:** betrayal, jealousy, heartbreak, moving on.

## Characters

## How to Run

### Play in the browser

Open **https://season-of-us-hc.netlify.app/**. No install needed.

### Run locally

1. Install the [Ren'Py SDK](https://www.renpy.org/latest.html) (Windows, macOS, or Linux).
2. Get the project, either by cloning it:
   ```sh
   git clone https://github.com/himanshusapkota/seasons-of-us.git
   ```
   or by clicking **Code → Download ZIP** on GitHub and extracting it.
3. Open the Ren'Py Launcher.
4. Select the `seasons-of-us` folder from the project list.
5. Click **Launch Project**.
