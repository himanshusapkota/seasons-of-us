
# Season of Us

Season of Us follows two high school students who fall in love in Grade 8. At first, their relationship is happy, and they believe they will stay together for a long time.

In Grade 9, everything changes when the boy catches the girl with another boy. Heartbroken, he becomes devastated and desperately asks her to return to his life. Eventually, she agrees, and they try to rebuild their relationship.

By Grade 10, their relationship seems happy again, but the pressure of the board examinations begins to affect them. The boy is the school's top student and spends most of his time studying, leaving little time for the girl. She becomes jealous when she sees other couples spending time together and eventually decides to break up with him.

However, during the examinations, the two are seated next to each other. The girl secretly decides to use the boy's answers to improve her grades. When the results are announced, the boy becomes the school topper, while the girl finishes as the second topper.

After Grade 10, they move to another school for further studies. They hope this will be a fresh beginning, but their relationship faces another problem. The girl develops feelings for another boy and betrays him again.

The boy is left heartbroken, realizing that the person he loved has chosen someone else once again. As their lives move forward, he must face the painful reality that sometimes love alone is not enough to keep two people together.
